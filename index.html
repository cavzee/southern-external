THE SOUTHERN EXTERNAL APPROACH

Property maintenance done properly.

Southern External provides structural and exterior property maintenance, focusing on the repair and upkeep of buildings, masonry and external property features.

Our work includes brickwork, masonry, boundary walls, fencing, paving and small exterior builds. We support homeowners, landlords, letting agents, property managers and facilities management companies across Reading and the Thames Valley.

  • Brick pillars & small builds

03BOUNDARIES

Boundary & Fencing Repairs

Repairs to boundary walls and fencing, including storm-damaged panels, posts and gates for residential and managed properties.
